§01 — Final year researchOct 2025 → Jul 2026 · 1st position

From banana peel
to packaging.

Petroleum plastics outlive the products they wrap. I developed a biodegradable film from gelatinised banana-peel starch, plasticised with glycerin and reinforced with titanium dioxide nanoparticles, and tuned the formulation until it was both strong and able to break down.

01 / Synthesis

Extract, gelatinise, vary

Extracted and gelatinised banana-peel starch, then systematically varied glycerin concentration and TiO₂ loading to optimise structural integrity.

02 / Testing

Every batch, every property

Tested 100% of film batches for tensile strength, moisture content, water swelling and biodegradability.

03 / Characterisation

Proof at the molecular level

FTIR confirmed polymer-network formation and the starch–glycerin–TiO₂ interactions behind the film's strength.

Fruit waste can become a mechanically sound, biodegradable film, with lower water solubility and swelling, and a real shot at sustainable packaging.

§02 — Field studyMar 2025 · Industrial canal, Faisalabad

What the canal
is carrying.

I sampled wastewater from a canal receiving discharge from several industries and ran a physicochemical workup. The verdict: slightly acidic, heavy in dissolved salts and ionic load, moderately hard. Water that needs treatment before it reaches the environment.
